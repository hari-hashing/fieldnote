PRIVACY

Newsletter privacy

Subscribing is voluntary and requires a confirmation link sent to the address supplied.

What is stored

The newsletter service stores your email address, selected IANA time zone, preferred Wednesday morning delivery hour, consent timestamp, confirmation state and the week of the last digest sent. The time zone is used to schedule delivery in your local time.

Service providers

The proposed deployment uses Cloudflare Workers and D1 for subscription data, and Resend for confirmation and weekly digest emails. The administrator must configure these services before subscriptions are activated.

Retention and control

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every weekly email. Unsubscribed records are retained as a suppression record to prevent accidental resubscription. Contact the maintainer through the public repository to request deletion.

Cookies and tracking

Theme and font preferences are stored locally in your browser. No account is required to browse the atlas.

Contact

For questions, open an issue in the public repository.
